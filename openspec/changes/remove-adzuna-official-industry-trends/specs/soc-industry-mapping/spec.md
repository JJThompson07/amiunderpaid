## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Canonical SIC and NAICS Industry Taxonomy

The system SHALL define a canonical, code-owned industry taxonomy (`shared/utils/industries.ts`) where every industry maps to a UK SIC 2007 section (with its ONS regular-pay CDID triplet) and/or a US NAICS sector or BLS supersector (with its BLS CES series ID). Non-industry job types (such as admin, sales, graduate, and part-time) MUST be excluded from the taxonomy.

#### Scenario: Fetching categories for a country

- **WHEN** `GET /api/market-data/categories` is called with `country=gb` or `country=us`
- **THEN** the endpoint SHALL return the canonical industries for that country from `shared/utils/industries.ts` without making any external API request.

### Requirement: Deterministic SOC-to-Industry Resolution on Every Search

The system SHALL deterministically map a resolved UK SOC 2020 code or US SOC 2018 code to its most likely industry (`shared/utils/socIndustryMap.ts`) on the salary and benchmark flows, using a static table generated by `scripts/build-soc-industry-map.ts` from the official occupation-by-industry source verified in pre-flight (UK: ONS occupation × SIC section employment; US: BLS National Employment Matrix), without requiring a live job-board response. The jobs page does not use this mapping.

#### Scenario: Resolving industry when live job listings are empty or unavailable

- **WHEN** a search resolves to UK SOC `2134` ("Programmers and software development professionals") or US SOC `15-1252` ("Software Developers") and the live job provider returns zero listings
- **THEN** the system SHALL still resolve the search's industry to `it-jobs` ("IT & Communications").

#### Scenario: Hierarchical fallback for 3-digit or 2-digit SOC prefixes

- **WHEN** `resolveIndustryFromSoc` is called with a 4-digit UK SOC or 6-digit US SOC that has no exact-code override
- **THEN** it SHALL resolve the industry from the code's 3-digit minor group, or its 2-digit sub-major/major group
- **AND** a code with no match at any level SHALL resolve to `null` rather than a guessed industry.
