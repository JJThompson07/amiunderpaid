## Why

Currently, the homepage consists primarily of static text and a search form. Search engines like Googlebot do not interact with forms, meaning they cannot discover or index the thousands of deep, data-rich `/salary/[title]/[country]` pages generated by the platform. This creates an "Orphan Page" spider trap that severely limits organic traffic. Additionally, the homepage's `<h1>` is purely branded ("Am I Underpaid?") rather than keyword-optimised.

## What Changes

- Add a new `SectionAmITrendingSearches` component to the bottom of the homepage.
- The component will contain a grid of `<NuxtLink>` tags pointing to highly searched roles (e.g., Software Engineer, Project Manager).
- The list of trending roles will be region-aware, meaning it will route to the correct US or UK parameters based on the current site's locale.
- Optimise the semantic heading structure of the hero section by injecting an SEO-friendly `<h2>` (or `<h1>` replacement) incorporating targeted keywords (e.g., "UK Salary Checker & Market Pay Calculator").

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `homepage-seo`: Enhancing the homepage with semantic SEO heading optimisations and an internal linking component (Trending Searches) to provide crawl paths for search engines.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

- `app/components/Brand/AmI/Home.vue` will include the new component.
- `app/components/Section/AmI/Hero.vue` will have modified heading semantics.
- New component `app/components/Section/AmI/TrendingSearches.vue` will be created.
